The Process

Your Brand Becomes The Store

From first briefing to live TakeOver in under 3 weeks. Every surface. Every cup. Every moment.

01

Briefing

We meet with your team for 30 min. We learn your brand DNA, campaign goals, and ideal customer experience.

02

Design & Simulator

Designers generate 3 photorealistic TakeOver concepts within 48-72 hours using the TakeOver Simulator.

03

Install & Refine

Magnetic fascia panels, LED strips, snap-in rails, cup runs — all swapped in 15 minutes at each store.

04

Launch

Your TakeOver goes live. AR triggers, QR codes, loyalty integration, and data capture all activate.

05

Monitor

Real-time dashboard: impressions, foot traffic, QR scans, AR dwell time, social mentions.
